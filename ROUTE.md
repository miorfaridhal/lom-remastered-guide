## 1. #1 Niccolo's Business Unusual

- **Land:** Domina · **Arc:** Niccolo
- **Starts:** Niccolo. Domina: leave the first area by the top path; he is in the next area
- **Needs:** None. The first quest you can take.
- **Gives:** Flame, Medallion (Wheel handed over at the start)
- **Ends:** Pick 'Throw Candy' to start the bandit fight on Luon Highway. Niccolo then gives you the Flame and the Medallion

1. Place the Colorblocks artifact directly above Home to create Domina, then enter it. Ignore the opening conversation and leave the first area by the top path.
2. Talk to Niccolo in the next area. Tell him you are 'Not Afraid' of the bandits, then answer Yes when he asks for help.
3. Niccolo wants Teapo. Go back to the town centre near the entrance and enter the bottom-most door of the lower-left building. Teapo is the one shaped like a teapot.
4. Talk to Teapo. Niccolo gives you the Wheel artifact. Leave the building and exit the area by the bottom path.
5. On the world map place the Wheel directly left of Home to create Luon Highway, then enter it.
6. Follow the road to the bandits.
   - Path: Right, Right, Upper Right at the fork, Right, Right
7. Choose 'Throw Candy' when asked. This starts the first boss fight. Afterwards Niccolo hands over the Flame and the Medallion.

## 2. #3 The Wisdom of Gaia

- **Land:** Domina · **Arc:** Faerie
- **Starts:** Daena. Domina inn (upper-left door in the town centre): bottom floor, room on the right
- **Needs:** Luon Highway placed (Wheel from #1)
- **Gives:** None
- **Ends:** Take Daena to Gaia on Luon Highway. Daena gives you the Forbidden Ring
- **Warning:** Highly missable. Tell Daena souls live on, then agree to take her. Do it before entering Gato Grottoes after Flame of Hope, and before clearing both #1 and #5.

1. In Domina, enter the inn (upper-left door in the town centre) and open the room on the right on the bottom floor.
2. Talk to Daena and answer that 'Souls are Forever'. Then say you will go with her to see Gaia. She joins your party.
3. Enter Luon Highway and walk to Gaia.
   - Path: Right, Right, Lower Right at the fork, Right, Right, Right
4. Talk to Gaia. Daena gives you the Forbidden Ring, which shares experience with the whole party.

## 4. #8 Huntin' Du'Cate

- **Land:** Jungle · **Arc:** Misc
- **Starts:** None (enter the Jungle). Place the Medallion directly above Mekiv Caverns to create the Jungle. Talk to Sotherbee, then follow the Lilipea
- **Needs:** Jungle placed (Medallion)
- **Gives:** Sand Rose, Ancient Tablet
- **Ends:** Beat Du'Cate at the end of the route. Give the 500 Lucre to the others to receive the Sand Rose
- **Warning:** Missed if Catchin' Lilipeas (#64) is cleared first. Fails if you start #64 while this is active.

2. Talk to Sotherbee in the first area. Go Right and come straight back to try to catch the Lilipea.
3. Go Right, talk to the Lilipea and keep following it. It leads to a man who casts a spell so you do not get lost. The map is easy to get lost in.
4. Back to the entrance.
   - Path: Right, Right, Upper Right, Left
5. Throne Room detour: from the entrance go Right, Right, Right. Talk to Hamson and follow him, talk to him again and follow him. Talk to Skippie and go Upper Left into the Throne Room. Talk to the character at the throne, then go Down and Lower Left. You are warped back to the entrance.
6. From the entrance go Right and talk to Skippie to help him. Go Up and talk to Hamson to help him, then go Left.
7. Reach the boss.
   - Path: Right, Right, Right, Lower Right, Right, Right, Right
8. Beat Du'Cate. You win 500 Lucre; choose to give the money to the others to receive the Sand Rose.

5. Find Pearl.
   - Path: Lower Right, Lower Right, Right, Lower Left, Lower Left, Left
6. Beat the Yeti to end the event.

   - Path: Right, Right, Lower Right, Right
7. A letter lies at the fork. Tell Capella to read it. Go Lower Left, then Left to find Diddle, and tell him Capella read the letter.
8. Enter the caves.
   - Path: Lower Left, Lower Left, talk to Capella, Upper Left
9. Beat Giga Rex. Keep the Gator Skin for Niccolo's Business Unusual: Part 4.

## 7. #56 The Mana Orchards

- **Land:** Home · **Arc:** Crafting
- **Starts:** Gaia, then Trent. Gaia on Luon Highway mentions Trent. Then go out your front door and take the path to the Lower Left
- **Needs:** Luon Highway placed (#1)
- **Gives:** None
- **Ends:** Beat the four Lullabuds, give Trent seeds, wait for the produce to ripen, pick it and put it in the big wooden box

2. Beat the four Lullabuds. Trent grows out of the ground.
3. Talk to Trent and give him seeds.
4. Wait a few game days for his produce to ripen. To speed it up, cross lands on the world map: each land crossed passes one day.
5. Stand under the produce you want and pick it.
6. Put the produce in the big wooden box to finish the event.

## 19. #30 The Spirits' Light

- **Land:** Lumina · **Arc:** Gilbert
- **Starts:** Monique. Place the Firefly Lamp directly left of Mekiv Caverns to create Lumina. Monique's Lamp Shop is in the Shopping District (north, upper right)
- **Needs:** Lumina placed (Firefly Lamp)
- **Gives:** Trembling Spoon, Sand Rose
- **Ends:** Sell the three lamps to the dudbears, then finish the scenes at the Lamp Shop

1. Place the Firefly Lamp directly left of Mekiv Caverns to create Lumina, then enter. The west section is the tavern and the north section is the Shopping District.
2. Go north to the Shopping District and to the Upper Right. Enter Monique's Lamp Shop and talk to her. Gilbert appears and offers to help her sell six lamps.
3. Leave the shop and talk to Gilbert. He buys three lamps and you sell the other three.
4. Go to the tavern in the western part of Lumina and talk to the puzzle-piece guy. He teaches you the Dudbear language.
5. Sell your three lamps to the dudbears by answering their questions.
